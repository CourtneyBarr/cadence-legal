Privacy Policy

Cadence · Last updated: August 16, 2026

Cadence is a personal content-scheduling tool built and used by a single owner to post to her own Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data Cadence handles and how it is used.

1. Who Uses Cadence

Cadence has exactly one user: its owner. It is not a multi-tenant service, and it does not collect, process, or store data about any other individual. There are no other user accounts on the system.

2. What Data Cadence Handles

  • OAuth access tokens for the owner's own Instagram and TikTok accounts, issued through each platform's official authorization flow. These tokens are used only to schedule and publish the owner's content and are never shared with any third party.
  • Scheduled post content and media (captions, images, video files, posting times) — stored only for as long as needed to publish the post to the platform it was scheduled for.

Cadence does not collect any data about, or shared by, any other individual.

3. How Data Is Stored

Access tokens and credentials are stored securely (environment/keychain-level secrets storage) and are never written into source code, logs, or version control. Access is limited to the application processes that need them to publish scheduled posts.

4. What Cadence Does Not Do

  • No advertising or ad-tracking.
  • No sale, rental, or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No analytics or telemetry beyond what is strictly needed to run the scheduler (e.g. logging a post's publish status for troubleshooting).
  • No collection of data about other Instagram or TikTok users, followers, or accounts the owner interacts with.

5. Third-Party Platforms

Cadence connects to Instagram and TikTok via their official APIs. Each platform's own privacy policy governs how they handle data on their end. Cadence's role is limited to authenticating as the owner and publishing content the owner has scheduled.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Scheduled post content is retained only until it has been published (or the schedule is cancelled). Access tokens are retained until revoked by the owner or the connected platform. The owner can request deletion of any stored data at any time via the contact below.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Cadence evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact@cadence.app.
