Privacy Policy

Cadence Scheduler · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Cadence Scheduler ("Cadence") helps people and brands plan and publish content to their own Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts, read and answer the messages and comments those accounts receive, and keep an eye on their own Meta ads. This policy explains what data Cadence handles, why, and the choices you have. Cadence is operated by Lucky Rabbit MGMT LLC, 8164 Sierra Madre Dr W, Jacksonville, Florida 32217.

1. Who uses Cadence

Cadence is used by account owners and the team members they invite. Each customer has a private workspace. A person or business may only connect accounts that they own or are authorized to manage. Workspaces are separated from each other, and one customer cannot see another customer's data.

2. Data Cadence handles

  • Account data: your name, email address, a one-way hash of your password, and sign-in session records.
  • Connection credentials: access tokens issued by Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Meta Ads through their official sign-in flows. Tokens are encrypted before they are stored.
  • Content you provide: images, videos, captions, brand descriptions, topic notes, posting times and approval decisions.
  • Data about your connected accounts: the name and identifiers of your Facebook Page and Instagram account, the permissions you granted, and the posts Cadence publishes for you.
  • Direct messages: when you switch messaging on for an account, Cadence receives the messages sent to your connected Facebook Page or Instagram account: the sender's name or username, the message text, links to attachments, and timestamps. They are stored so you can read them and reply.
  • Comments: when you switch comments on for an account, Cadence receives comments and replies on your own posts: the commenter's name or username, the comment text, the post it was left on, and timestamps. They are stored so you can read, answer, hide, like or delete them.
  • Ad account data: when you connect a Meta ad account, Cadence reads its campaigns, ad sets, ads, budgets and performance figures, so you can see them and pause, resume or change a budget when you choose.
  • Operational records: an audit log of connections, actions sent to Meta and TikTok, errors, and the dates they happened.

3. How Cadence uses data

  • To schedule and publish the content you approve to the accounts you connect.
  • To show you your messages and comments and to send the replies you write.
  • To show you your ad performance and carry out the ad changes you ask for.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent duplicate or excessive actions, and investigate problems.

Cadence does not use data it receives from Meta or TikTok for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than providing these features to the account owner.

4. AI generation

If you use Cadence to generate video, the prompt you write and the style notes on the shelf are sent to the generation service you choose, currently Kling. Cadence does not send messages, comments, customer names or ad data to any AI service. If that changes, this policy will be updated first. The service's own terms govern how it handles what it receives.

5. Actions are taken by a person

  • Every reply, private message, like, hide, delete and ad change happens only when a signed-in person presses the button for it. Cadence does not reply, like, hide or message on its own.
  • Cadence uses only the official Meta and TikTok APIs. It does not use browser automation, scraping or any method designed to get around a platform's restrictions.
  • Cadence does not follow or unfollow accounts, send bulk or promotional messages, or buy or sell likes, followers or comments.
  • Replies to messages are sent only within the 24-hour window Meta allows. Reply and message rates are limited per account to protect it.
  • Publishing, messaging and comments are each off until you switch them on for an account, and you can switch any of them off at any time.

6. Who receives data

Cadence does not sell personal data. Data is shared only with:

  • The platforms you connect (Meta and TikTok), as needed to carry out what you ask.
  • Service providers that run Cadence: Vercel (hosting), Neon (database), Cloudflare (file storage and scheduling), Resend (email), and Kling if you use video generation.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

7. Security

Access tokens are encrypted at rest with keys that can be rotated, and are never written to logs. Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. Sign-in sessions are stored as hashed tokens. Requests from Meta to Cadence's webhooks are verified with a signature before they are accepted. No system is perfectly secure, and Cadence will notify affected customers of a breach as the law requires.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Connection tokens are deleted when you disconnect an account.
  • Messages and comments from an account are deleted when you disconnect that account. Until then they are kept so you can use them, and you can ask for earlier deletion.
  • Content you upload is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete it.
  • Audit records are kept for as long as your workspace exists.
  • Backups of the database may hold deleted data for a short time until older backups are replaced.

9. Your choices and data deletion requests

You can disconnect any account and delete your content from the Cadence dashboard. To ask us to access, correct or delete personal data, including data about a person who messaged or commented on a connected account, email info@luckyrabbitmgmt.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you have removed Cadence from your Facebook, Instagram or TikTok settings and want the rest of the data we hold deleted, use the instructions at cadence-dusky-ten.vercel.app/data-deletion.html. We delete the data within 30 days of confirming the request.

California residents: Cadence does not sell or share personal information, and you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and expect equal service if you do.

10. Children

Cadence is for businesses and adults. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes in a way that affects how data is used, we will update the date above and notify workspace owners. We will not expand how data from a connected platform is used without any approval that platform requires.
